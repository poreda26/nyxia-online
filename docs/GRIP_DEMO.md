# Silah tutuşu örneği

Tek Warrior, Közdiş ve Gökyarık için önceden çizilmiş iki tam poz. Demo: public/grip-demo.html. Görsel: public/demos/warrior-grip-v1.png. Ana oyuna bağlı değildir; localStorage kullanmaz. CSS bekleme ve hamle hareketi, gerçek eklem animasyonu değildir. Silah değişiminde tüm poz değişir. Her yeni silah / zırh kombinasyonu için ek çizim gerekir; bu bir otomatik evrensel ekipman sistemi değildir. Kullanıcı onayı olmadan genişletilmez.

İlk şeffaf çıktılarda dama deseni piksellere işlendiği için örnek düz koyu fonda hazırlandı; haritalara uygulanmadan önce şeffaf sprite üretimi ayrıca çözülmelidir.

Üretim: built-in image_gen. Promptlar:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production game character comparison sprite atlas on actual transparent background, landscape 1536x1024. Exactly two equal 768x1024 cells side by side, no dividing line, no text. SAME full-body male human dark fantasy warrior in BOTH cells, identical face, short dark hair, steel armor with bronze trims, burgundy tabard, identical size and planted feet baseline at 94% canvas height. 3/4 view facing screen right, slight top-down perspective for a mobile turn-based RPG, hand-painted textured 2000s MMORPG remastered aesthetic. Leave generous 8% padding inside each cell around all limbs and weapon. LEFT cell warrior naturally grips the red hooked falchion in reference image 1 with TWO armored hands, in a battle ready pose angled diagonally upward to screen right. RIGHT cell the IDENTICAL warrior naturally grips the blue double crescent poleaxe from reference image 2, hands correctly separated along its long ivory wrapped shaft, blade upright toward screen right. Reference images are weapon design references, preserve their recognizable silhouettes and materials. Hands must visibly wrap grips, thumbs opposed, weapons behind fingers, straight wrists; no floating weapon, no blade through palm, no extra fingers or arms. Both poses convincingly bear weapon weight. No shields, no other weapons, no floor, no backdrop, no shadows outside figures. Complete unclipped head, feet, weapon tips. Clean silhouettes. This is one two-variant atlas for one playable warrior, not two different identities.

Edit this two-character sprite atlas. Keep BOTH warrior designs, faces, armor, hand grips and weapon shapes unchanged. Remove ALL checkerboard pixels and make the entire background ACTUAL alpha transparency, no white, no grey squares. Also shrink each entire character-and-weapon uniformly by 12% within its own half of canvas, so LEFT figure and sword are strictly inside x=35..733 and RIGHT figure and axe inside x=803..1501, with a clear transparent gutter at x=734..802. Both feet same baseline y=960. Canvas remains 1536x1024. Do not crop any body or weapon tip. Output transparent PNG.

Edit background only. Preserve both entire warriors, faces, hands, grips, weapons, poses, scale and positions EXACTLY. Replace the grey-and-white checkerboard everywhere with a perfectly uniform solid dark charcoal-teal #101b1c RGB(16,27,28). No checkerboard anywhere, no gradient, no texture, no transparency. Keep a two-cell 1536x1024 atlas. Do not move any character or any weapon.
