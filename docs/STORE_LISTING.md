## English

**Full description** (max 4000 characters)
```
Nyxia Online is an online RPG you carry in your pocket. Start as a Warrior, a Rogue (archer) or a Mage, clear six regions, take down bosses with your clan and climb the Warzone ranking.

THREE CLASSES, TWO RACES
The sturdy front-line Warrior, the ranged archer Rogue or the destructive Mage. Each class has its own skills; pick them, equip them and time them in battle.

SIX REGIONS, COUNTLESS MONSTERS
From Fallow Valley to the Crimson Battlefront, regions unlock as you level. Each one has its own monsters, a map-end boss and a daily solo dungeon.

CLANS AND TEAM BATTLES
Found a clan or join one. The clan dungeon, shared vault, clan building and clan boss give rewards you cannot reach alone. Lead your clan with leader, deputy and officer ranks.

GEAR, UPGRADES, SETS
Weapons and armor in six qualities, accessories and wings. Upgrade your items with scrolls, complete your sets and watch your character transform.

WARZONE
World bosses, an empowered monster hunt and a weekly ranking. The best players win diamonds.

PLAYER MARKET
Put your items on your stall and trade with other players.

A REASON TO COME BACK EVERY DAY
Daily login rewards, a daily wheel, daily and weekly quests and timed events. Optionally watch a short ad to earn free diamonds.

FAIR PLAY
Battles, rewards and the economy are calculated on the server and protected against cheating. Your progress is tied to your account: change phones and continue where you left off.

COMMUNITY
We run regular giveaways on Discord and Instagram. You can earn a reward once per platform from inside the game.

Note: The game is for ages 13 and up. Contains ads. You can sign up with Google or your email address.
```
