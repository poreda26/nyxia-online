What awaits you in the game?

Clan and boss battles

Found your clan and manage the shared vault. Fight together in the 20-stage clan dungeon and the clan boss.

Warzone

From level 50: world bosses, an empowered hunt and the weekly ranking.

Gear and upgrades

Weapons and armor in 6 qualities, accessories and wings. Upgrade your gear and complete your sets.

Player market

Put your items on your stall and trade with other players.

Always online

Your progress is stored on the server. Change phones and continue where you left off.

Fair play

Battles and rewards are calculated on the server and protected against cheating.
