Dungeons and bosses

Daily solo dungeon

Every map has its own dungeon: 6 stages, each tougher than the last, ending with the dungeon lord. You get 3 entries a day, and an extra entry costs 150 diamonds. The game reminds you of your entries and asks for confirmation before you enter.

Map-end bosses

Defeat enough of a region's monsters to unlock its boss. It can be defeated once a day and rewards gold, experience and a guaranteed Guardian Chest. The game asks for confirmation before you enter, because it is tough.

Clan dungeon

Your clan's shared 20-stage dungeon. A sentinel awaits at stage 10 and the dungeon lord at stage 20. Each clan gets 2 entries a day.

The dungeon drops clan materials: Wood Silver Iron Gold Bar. They are used to upgrade the clan building and vault.

Clan boss

When the clan building level and the clan's accumulated NP (national points) are high enough, the leader, an officer or the deputy can open the clan boss. The boss is defeated within a one-hour window by the combined hits of all members.
