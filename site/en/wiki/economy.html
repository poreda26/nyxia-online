Economy and diamonds

Gold and diamonds

Gold is earned in the game: by hunting, quests and selling items. Diamonds are the premium currency; they are earned from the daily login, the weekly ranking and by watching ads, and can be bought through Apple/Google once store purchases open (diamonds are verified on the server and added to your account).

Scheduled events

Noon EXP Rush: every day at 12:30 for 10 minutes; an experience bonus builds up during the event.

Premium

Premium lasts 15 days and gives advantages including auto-battle. Premium bought in the store arrives as a voucher in your inventory; use it from your inventory or bank whenever you like to start it. The 15-day vouchers bought in the store can be sold on the market. Short vouchers from the wheel and community rewards cannot be traded.

Wings and boosts

Wings give a look and small bonuses (5 models, 2.000 diamonds each): Dawn Guardian Frost Phoenix Crimson Ashes Twilight Emerald Oath

Boost scrolls last 30 minutes and are bought in packs of 20.

Account and free diamonds

You start with 2 character slots; the 3rd slot costs 500 diamonds.
