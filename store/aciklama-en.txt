Nyxia Online is a mobile online RPG you carry in your pocket. Start as a Warrior, a Rogue (archer) or a Mage, clear six regions, take down bosses with your clan, duel in the Warzone and climb the weekly ranking. One account, the same progress on every phone.

THREE CLASSES, TWO RACES, YOUR BUILD
The sturdy front-line Warrior, the ranged archer Rogue or the destructive Mage. Each class has its own skills: learn them, line them up on your battle bar and time them right.

SIX REGIONS, COUNTLESS MONSTERS AND BOSSES
From Fallow Valley to the Crimson Battlefront, six regions unlock as you level. Each one has its own monsters, a map-end boss and a daily solo dungeon. Attack in turn-based battles, use skills, drink potions and beat dangerous bosses.

BUILD A CLAN, GROW STRONGER TOGETHER
Found a clan or join one. The 20-stage clan dungeon, the shared vault, the clan building and the clan boss give rewards you cannot reach alone. Lead your clan with leader, deputy and officer ranks and chat with your friends.

WARZONE AND PvP
World bosses, an empowered monster hunt and player duels. Reach the top of the weekly ranking, the best players win diamonds. You can also challenge a friend to a friendly match with nothing at stake.

GEAR, UPGRADES AND SETS
Weapons and armor in six qualities, accessories and wings. Upgrade your items with scrolls up to +8. If you succeed the item gets stronger, if you fail the item and the scroll are lost: you choose the risk. Complete your sets and watch your character transform.

PLAYER MARKET
Put your items on your stall and trade with other players. The item you are looking for may be on another player's stall.

A REASON TO COME BACK EVERY DAY
Daily login rewards, a daily wheel, daily and weekly quests, a monster book and timed events. Optionally watch a short ad to earn free diamonds.

FAIR PLAY
Battles, rewards and the economy are calculated on the server and protected against cheating. Your progress is tied to your account: change phones and continue where you left off. A guided tutorial helps you with your first steps.

TURKISH AND ENGLISH
Play in Turkish or English, you can switch the language in Settings.

COMMUNITY
We run regular giveaways on Discord and Instagram. You can earn a reward once per platform from inside the game.

Note: The game is for ages 13 and up. Free to download, contains ads. You can sign up with Google or your email address.
